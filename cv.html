Education

PhD in Astronomy and Physics

Radboud University | Nov 2013 - Aug 2018

Supervisors: P.J. Groot, T. Prince (Caltech), S.R. Kulkarni (Caltech)

Thesis: "Fast optical variability with the Palomar Transient Factory"

For my PhD thesis, I used data from the Palomar Transient Factory (PTF) to determine the rate of intra-night transients in preparation for the search for kilonovae. In addition, I worked on data-mining PTF to find short-period white dwarf binary stars.
